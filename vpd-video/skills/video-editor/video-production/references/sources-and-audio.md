# Recording, assets and audio

Use the creator's supplied preferences privately. GarageBand, Screen Studio, MacWhisper, ChatGPT/image generation and Suno are optional tools, not requirements or evidence of a working integration. Existing media can enter this workflow without new recordings or new accounts.

## Project home and originals

Choose one project home before production writes begin. Start small and expand as needed:

```text
project/
  brief.md           # links to current writing and accepted choices
  writing/           # narration, scene plan, vocabulary
  sources/           # original video, voice and music, or indexed archive links
  assets/            # reference sheets, selected art, prompts and provenance
  edit/              # renderer source, raw/working transcripts, take windows, timing
  review/            # candidate manifest, player and portable media
  exports/v001/      # delivered media and verification receipt
```

Keep native recording/editor projects and original exports unchanged. Copy or explicitly index intake files from Downloads or recording-app export folders; do not delete originals after importing. Record source content digests, selected source windows, derivatives, model/settings where relevant, and actual output paths. Filenames such as “latest” or “final” are not proof of the selected take.

Use project-relative paths for portable review bundles. Version scripts, manifests and recipes; store large media in the chosen archive with clear references and suitable ignores. Do not put private creator profiles, client media or user-level settings in public examples. Stop obsolete preview processes when their work is finished; retain review files and version history.

## Narration and recorded footage

Make a short test recording, export it, then listen before recording the full narration. Check clipping, room tone, input choice and unwanted metronome/click tracks. Use consistent room/microphone settings across planned takes; a pickup may still sound different after level matching. If a rerecording is needed, name the audible issue rather than applying a universal cleanup recipe.

Record at a comfortable pace. After mistakes, leave separation and repeat a complete phrase so edits retain consonants, context and breath. A scratch read belongs inside scriptwriting and may become the final take. Keep original M4A/WAV and native sessions; a practical PCM working derivative can simplify editing but does not improve the original's quality by itself. Capture independent screen, camera and microphone tracks when available.

Use [local transcription and caching](transcription.md). Keep raw recognition output, corrected wording, selected take windows and remaining uncertainty distinct. Measure the selected read including its intended pauses. Reconcile what was actually said with visible copy before timing the final scenes.

## Generated pictures and animated assets

Use the available image-generation skill/tool when generating or editing images, whether through ChatGPT or another authorized provider. Start with accepted references and one representative style/scene test. Describe poses and layers for the action they must support; prove that the crucial scene can read before generating a large manifest of parts.

Preserve prompts, original generations, chosen references, selected outputs and their provenance. Reuse approved character references for later images. To remove a background or adjust a prop, preserve the accepted identity instead of unnecessarily regenerating the person. Inspect edges, transparency and consistency on the actual scene background.

Use editable type for exact names, values, stamps, terminal output and cover lettering when generated text is unreliable. Physical objects should communicate their role: a magazine needs recognizable cover/page treatment, not merely a blank rectangle. Art serves the story's visible actions and reading order; it is not an asset-count target.

## Suno and music candidates

Suno can provide music candidates through the user's existing workflow; a manual exported-file handoff is valid. Do not assume an unofficial generation API or claim to have generated music from an imported file. Save the musical brief/prompt, candidate ID, original export, available source/usage information and eventual choice beside the project.

Explore mood early; establish final coverage after measuring the narration, picture holds and ending. Probe every supplied candidate's actual duration. If music is short, report the gap and propose a new generation/extension or a specifically designed edit. Do not silently loop, stretch or truncate away a necessary ending. Preserve an authorized ending or count-in only when it belongs to this project.

For A/B auditions, keep identical narration and picture, apply comparable mix treatment, and measure the finished music beds over the same relevant span. Match perceived levels with listening; a loudness number alone does not judge energy, masking or a good musical ending. Export premixed videos for each candidate instead of synchronizing independently playing browser audio and video.

## Sound review

Use waveform/silence, loudness and peak tools for useful measurements. Use your available listening capability for voice consistency, clicks, edit edges, intelligibility and music masking. Preserve intentional pauses and breaths; a detector can suggest edit boundaries but cannot choose the performance.

Keep gains, fades, ducking/carving and cleanup recipes with each mix. Use processing appropriate to the recording; never import another project's metronome-removal settings, fixed loudness target or fade lengths as universal defaults. For a music-only revision, retain the accepted voice and picture and verify what was preserved. Report actual listening separately from technical checks.
